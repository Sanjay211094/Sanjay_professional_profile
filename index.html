About me

I've spent 9+ years in business intelligence and analytics, moving from hands-on reporting at Maersk to leading the BI and analytics function at Foodhub Software Solutions.

My foundation is Power BI and advanced SQL: data modeling, cleansing and transformation, query optimization across enterprise data warehouses, and dashboards tuned for performance, usability and scale. Today I also review and guide the work of BI developers across the full BI ecosystem, from desktop to server and cloud.

Where descriptive reporting isn't enough, I build machine learning and GenAI solutions: churn and demand forecasting, anomaly and fraud detection, and hybrid LLM pipelines that analyze consumer chats and B2B calls.

I work directly with the COO, CEO and cross-functional leaders, translating their priorities into KPI frameworks, an analytics roadmap and governed BI strategy. I lead a team of BI analysts, data engineers and ML engineers through a structured weekly skill-upgrade cadence, backed by validation controls that keep every dashboard reconciled to the backend.
